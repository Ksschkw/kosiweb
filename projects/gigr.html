01Overview

A hyper-local services marketplace with a cryptographic trust and escrow layer, built in the repository named BAROS. Clients hold money in escrow until a job is completed to satisfaction, and providers build a reputation that cannot be quietly edited later.

All chain interaction is hidden behind an ordinary web experience, so neither side has to understand wallets or gas to use it.

02How it is built

  • Escrow releases on completion, which removes the risk of non-payment for providers and of non-delivery for clients.
  • Reputation is minted as compressed NFTs, so a vouch is permanent and attributable to the person who gave it.
  • PostgreSQL with PostGIS handles proximity matching, so local work reaches the people who can actually reach it.
  • An AI assistant can post jobs and negotiate on the user's behalf, and disputes route to a community jury.
  • The front end is a progressive web app with a backend orchestrator, and the product is currently in pilot.
