01Overview

An agentic intent solver for the Injective ecosystem with hardware-enforced execution, built as a bounty submission. The design separates intelligence from execution on principle: the part that reasons holds no keys.

The intelligence layer reads live chain state, watches pending staking rewards and constructs the payload, then hands it to a Ledger device where a person reviews and clear-signs before anything is broadcast.

02How it is built

  • Zero private keys in the agent process, so a compromised model cannot move funds on its own.
  • Human approval happens on the hardware device rather than in the application, which is the difference between a prompt and a guarantee.
  • Includes a staking auto-compounder that withdraws and re-delegates rewards, and a buy-back trigger that constructs a commitment payload.
  • Built against the Injective MCP servers for live chain queries.
