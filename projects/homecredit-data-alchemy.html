HomeCredit Data Alchemy

Credit risk modelling on the Home Credit dataset, covering feature engineering across bureau and previous-application history through to model evaluation.

Machine Learning Risk
Code

01Overview

A data preparation pipeline for the Home Credit default risk problem, turning seven raw relational tables into six domain-specific datasets built for modelling.

Most of the work is in the transformation: relational history becomes behaviour a model can actually use.

02How it is built

  • Feature engineering driven by what the data means for credit risk rather than by what is easy to compute.
  • Memory footprint reduced substantially without discarding information.
  • Behavioural signals derived from mobile usage and application history.
