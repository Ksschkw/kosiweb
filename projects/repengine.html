01Overview

A freelance marketplace and reputation-weighted governance platform built to price trust cryptographically instead of trusting a profile that nobody verified.

Job access and voting power are both gated on an on-chain reputation score, so a new wallet cannot buy its way into governance on day one.

02How it is built

  • Clients can require a minimum reputation score for a listing, which makes the gate enforceable rather than advisory.
  • Escrow backs the contract between both sides, so payment and delivery are linked.
  • Governance weight follows reputation, so influence has to be earned through completed work.
  • Full-stack, deployed, and built with mobile use in mind.
